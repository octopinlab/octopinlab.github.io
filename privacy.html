OctoPin Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

"OctoPin Analytics" is a personal tool used only by the owner of the OctoPin YouTube channel. No one else uses it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel: it uploads videos the owner has approved and reads the channel's video and analytics statistics.

What it does not do

It reads only counts and totals (views, likes, comment counts, subscriber count, watch time). It does not read comment text or commenter names, and it does not collect, sell, share or disclose data about any other person or user. It does not use data for advertising.

Data we keep

Only the owner's own channel statistics and the OAuth access tokens. Tokens are never published. All of it is deleted when the owner revokes access, or on request to the contact address below.

Storage

Tokens and statistics are stored on a private computer controlled by the owner and are not shared with any third party. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
